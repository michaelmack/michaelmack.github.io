We are excited to publish a [new paper in *Hippocampus*](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/share/author/Y8A2KAMIRCV4MNMA2DRS?target=10.1002/hipo.23382) on the relationship between hippocampal circuitry and category learning. We find that the density of white matter connections between hippocampal subfields CA3 and CA1 relate to individual differences in category exception learning. This work comes from our first MRI study conducted at the U of T Psychology Department's [Toronto Neuroimaging Facility](http://toni.psych.utoronto.ca). Data collection and analysis efforts were led by incoming graduate student Melisa Gumus and undergraduate student Teresa Zhu and the project was conducted in collaboration with [Meg Schlichting](http://buddingmindslab.utoronto.ca).

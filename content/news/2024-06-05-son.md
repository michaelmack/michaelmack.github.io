Dr. Gaeun Son successfully defended her PhD thesis! Gaeun's thesis included all her amazing work linking latent spaces of generative AI models to human perception. It is a true accomplishment! Gaeun's next move is a postdoctoral position in Soojin Park's lab at Yonsei University. Gaeun will be dearly missed, but we are so excited for her! Congratulations, Dr. Son!

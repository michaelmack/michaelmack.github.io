Melisa Gumus recently published a longitudinal study showing that the prevalence of anxiety and depression is higher in patients with young-onset of Alzheimer’s than those with late-onset, and the difference remains significant over time: [https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007%2Fs11357-020-00304-y](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007%2Fs11357-020-00304-y). Congrats, Melisa!

Congratulations to Jason Chow for being awarded an OPAM Student Travel Award! Jason will be presenting a poster at [OPAM 2018](http://www.opam.net) on his recent work looking at the time course of categorization with object substitution masking: 

Chow, J.K., & Mack, M.L. (2017). Object substitution masking reveals a competitive dynamic between levels of categorization. *Poster presentation at the Object Perception, Attention, and Memory Annual Conference 2018*, Vancouver, B.C.
